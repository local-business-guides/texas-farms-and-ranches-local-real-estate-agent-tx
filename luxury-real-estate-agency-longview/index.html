Key facts and steps to expect

The service can cover buying, selling or investing in homes, farms, ranches, acreage and commercial properties. The work may include:

  • Talking through your goals and the type of property you have in mind.
  • Searching available listings and arranging showings.
  • Discussing pricing or marketing for a property you’re selling.
  • Supporting negotiations and the process from contract to closing.

There isn’t one standard timeline for every transaction. Timing can depend on the property, how quickly suitable options are found, the parties’ decisions and the steps required after a contract. Costs also vary by property and by the service arrangement. Ask providers to explain their applicable fees and terms before you proceed; no specific price should be assumed from a general overview.

A short glossary can make conversations easier. Acreage means a parcel of land measured in acres. Improvements are existing additions or features on a property. MLS is a listing system used by real estate professionals to share property information.
