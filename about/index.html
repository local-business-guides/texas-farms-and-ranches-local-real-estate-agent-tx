A Longview-based real estate agency

Texas Farms & Ranches helps buyers, sellers and investors with farms, ranches, acreage, homes and commercial properties across Texas. We also assist with residential and commercial rentals. Our work can include property searches, showings, pricing, marketing, negotiations and support through the steps from contract to closing.

With access to multiple Texas MLS markets, we help clients compare properties and consider details such as access, water, improvements and intended use. The right details depend on the property and what you plan to do with it, so you can talk with us about the questions on your mind.

Visit or contact us

Our Longview office is at 4138 US-259, Longview, TX 75605. Call +1903-663-5000 to discuss buying, selling or rental needs. We’re open Monday through Friday from 8:00 to 17:00, and closed Saturday and Sunday. You can call during business hours or stop by the office to speak with us about the kind of property you’re looking for.
