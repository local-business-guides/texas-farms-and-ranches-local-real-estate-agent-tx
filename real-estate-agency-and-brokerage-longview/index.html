What the service covers and what to expect

The work may begin with defining your search or sale goals, then reviewing properties or preparing a listing, arranging visits, discussing price and negotiating terms. For land and rural property, details such as access, water, existing improvements and intended use can shape the review. For a home or commercial property, different features and transaction needs may matter.

Key facts
- Coverage: Searches and transactions involving farms, ranches, acreage, homes and commercial property.
- Timing: The schedule varies with your search, property availability, decisions, negotiations and contract-to-closing steps. Ask what milestones apply to your situation.
- Cost factors: Costs can depend on the property, the service requested and the terms agreed for the transaction. Ask providers to explain their fees and any other expected expenses before moving forward.

A few terms you may hear: listing means a property offered for sale; showing is a scheduled visit to view it; MLS is a shared listing system used by real estate professionals; and closing is the final stage when the transaction is completed. Ask what each term means for your specific agreement.
